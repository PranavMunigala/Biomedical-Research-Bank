## Company Overview

- **Location:** New York City (per Dealroom/Tracxn listings)
- **Founded:** 2025 by David van Dijk (CEO, Yale professor) and Ivan Vrkic
- **Stage:** Seed; Y Combinator W2026
- **Mission:** an agentic drug company -- AI agents run the full drug discovery pipeline on biological foundation models that simulate human biology

## Technology & Products

- **Cell2Sentence (C2S):** turns a cell's gene expression into a ranked sequence of gene names so an LLM can train on it. Developed with Google DeepMind, ICML 2024. Analogy: tokenizing a document by sorting words by importance so an NLP model needs no new architecture.
- **C2S-Scale 27B:** 27B-parameter model built on Google's Gemma architecture. Generated a hypothesis that silmitasertib (CX-4945) is a conditional immune amplifier, boosting MHC-I antigen presentation ~50% in live-cell experiments.
- AI agents orchestrate pipeline steps; public architecture detail is thin, not independently verified.
- More: https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/celltype
